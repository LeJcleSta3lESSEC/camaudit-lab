# Lesson 04 — RTSP, ONVIF, and UPnP

RTSP can expose live video if misconfigured. ONVIF can expose metadata or stream URIs. UPnP can allow devices to ask routers to open ports.

Safe audit questions:

- Is RTSP open?
- Does it require authentication?
- Is ONVIF discovery enabled?
- Is UPnP enabled on the router?
- Are any camera services exposed to the internet?

Do not brute force. Do not capture feeds. Stop at exposure confirmation.
